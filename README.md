# 🏆 Champions Club — Fix Mix Sundays

Welcome to **Champions Club**, a recurring social padel community built around the Fix Mix format. We play every Sunday — rotating partners, competing for weekly points, and climbing a monthly leaderboard. Whether you're a seasoned player or just picking up a racket, there's a spot on court for you.

---

## What Is Fix Mix?

Fix Mix is a social padel format where players **rotate partners across multiple rounds** each session. Because you never play the whole session with the same partner, your individual performance — not who you're paired with — determines your score. It keeps things fair, fun, and social.

---

## The Points System

### Weekly Scoring

| Event | Points |
|---|---|
| Each game won | +1 pt |
| Attendance bonus (just showing up) | +5 pts |
| Session winner (most games won that Sunday) | +3 pts |

The attendance bonus matters: even on an off day, you bank 5 points for being there. That's what keeps people coming back week after week.

### Monthly Ranking

- Your weekly totals are summed across all Sundays in the month.
- Only your **best 3 sessions** count — the worst week is dropped.
- This means a missed Sunday or a bad round early in the month doesn't knock you out of contention. You can always fight back.

### Example Week

A player who wins 6 games, shows up, and tops the session:

| | |
|---|---|
| 6 games won | +6 pts |
| Attendance | +5 pts |
| Session winner | +3 pts |
| **Total** | **14 pts** |

---

## How It Works — Sunday to Sunday

1. **Show up** — earn your 5-point attendance bonus automatically.
2. **Play your rounds** — partners rotate each round; win games, earn points.
3. **Session winner** — the player with the most games won that day gets +3 bonus points.
4. **Leaderboard posted** — standings go out on WhatsApp/Instagram after each Sunday.
5. **Month closes** — best 3 weeks summed, prizes awarded.

## Community

Champions Club is social first. The points system is competitive enough to keep things interesting, but relaxed enough that every level of player enjoys it. New players are welcome any Sunday — no need to pre-register, just show up and play.

Follow the running leaderboard on our WhatsApp group and Instagram after each session.

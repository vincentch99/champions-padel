The Format

Fix Mix Sundays

Players rotate partners across several rounds — your individual performance is what counts, not who you're paired with.

👫

Fixed Mixed Pairs

Each team is one male and one female player, paired at the start of the month. Partners stay the same every Sunday — no swaps.

📊

Individual Scoring

Points are tracked per person. Winning a game earns you points regardless of who your partner was that round.

📅

Monthly Competition

Weekly totals add up across the month. Your best 3 sessions count — one bad Sunday won't knock you out of the running.

📍

New Venue Every Week

Location changes each Sunday — different courts, different vibes. Check the leaderboard each week to see where you're playing.

Scoring System

How Points Work

Designed to reward winning, reward showing up, and keep everyone in the race until the final Sunday.

📅 Weekly Points

  • Games Won (per game) +1 pt
  • Attendance Bonus +5 pts
  • Session Winner Bonus +3 pts
💡 Showing up guarantees 5 points even on a bad day — so there's always a reason to come back next week.

🏆 Monthly Ranking

  • Sessions counted per month Best 3
  • Worst session Dropped
  • Monthly total Sum of best 3
🎯 Drop-week rule means one missed or bad Sunday doesn't kill your month. You can still climb back.

Getting Started

How It Works

Simple to follow, every Sunday.

1

Sign Up & Get Paired

Register through Reclub and pay to confirm your spot. Once confirmed, you're matched into a mixed team (one male + one female) for the full month.

2

Show Up Sunday

Check the leaderboard for this week's venue — location changes every Sunday. Your team banks 5 attendance points just for being there.

3

Play & Earn Points

Win games, earn points as a team. The session-winning team gets a bonus +3. Leaderboard updates after every Sunday.

4

Month Closes

Your best 3 sessions are summed up. Worst week dropped. Prizes awarded across Top Champion, Most Improved, and Most Consistent.
